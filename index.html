How to play

Goal. Someone poisons Sir Edmund before midnight. Find out who, prove it, and break the time loop.

Controls. WASD / arrow keys to walk · E to talk or examine when a prompt appears · J journal · H this help · Esc close. Reading, talking and the journal pause the clock.

The loop. Each loop runs from 11:56 to midnight (four minutes). Then the clock strikes thirteen and everything resets. You keep your journal — evidence and everything you saw. Everyone else forgets and repeats the same routine. Items you picked up go back where they were.

The rival. The murderer also remembers the loops. He learns only what he sees you do, or what you show him. If he catches you investigating, he changes his plan next loop. Investigate quietly.

Saving Edmund. You can stop the poisoning: take the vial before it is used, interrupt the attempt, or take the glass away.

Proving it. Journal → Accusation. Choose the suspect, a motive (why tonight) and an action (evidence they actually did it). Owning a suspicious object is not proof.

On screen. Gold figure = you. Floating ? = worth examining. Glowing ring = what E will interact with.

Blackthorn Hall · North Yorkshire · 11:56 PM

The Thirteenth Chime

Twelve years ago, a young man died at Blackthorn Hall.
Tonight, someone intends to tell the truth.
Someone else intends to stop him.

A storm has cut the house off. At midnight, Sir Edmund Blackthorn will sign a statement admitting that the death of Daniel Price was covered up.

You are Morgan, a private investigator hired to witness it. Before midnight, someone will poison him.

Who's in the house

Sir Edmund BlackthornThe host. Signs his statement at midnight — if he lives.
Julian AshcroftThe family solicitor. Courteous, helpful, always nearby.
Thomas ReedThe butler. Prepares Edmund's evening drink.
Clara BlackthornEdmund's niece. The new will costs her dearly.
Dr Helen WardThe family doctor. Has access to medicines.

Everyone is hiding something. That does not mean everyone is a murderer.

How to win — three steps

  1. Find the motive. Read Edmund's statement on his desk in the Study. Who does it expose?
  2. Catch the act. Someone poisons Edmund's drink on the Study drink table just before midnight. See it with your own eyes.
  3. Accuse. Press the Accuse button and choose the suspect, the motive evidence and the action evidence. Suspicion is not enough — you need proof of both.

Bonus: save Edmund. After you see the poisoning, take the glass from the drink table before he drinks it.

The time loop

  • Each loop lasts four minutes: 11:56 to midnight.
  • At midnight the clock strikes thirteen and everything starts again at 11:56.
  • You keep your journal: every clue and everything you saw.
  • Everyone else forgets and repeats exactly the same routine — so you can learn it.
  • Objects you picked up go back where they were.
  • You cannot be everywhere at once. Use each loop to watch somewhere new.
  • Reading and talking pause the clock.

Someone else remembers

You are learning how he commits the murder.
He is learning how you investigate it.

  • The murderer remembers the loops too.
  • He only learns what he sees you do — or what you show him.
  • If he catches you investigating, he changes his plan next loop: a new hiding place, a different route.
  • Watch without being seen. The advantage belongs to whoever reveals less.
